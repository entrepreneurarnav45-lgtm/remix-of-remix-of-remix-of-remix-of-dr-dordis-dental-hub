# Remix of Remix of Remix of Remix of Dr Dordis Dental Hub

Create a modern, clean, and welcoming dentist portfolio and service introduction website. 

Key requirements:
1. Header & Hero: Warm dentist introduction, doctor portrait, tagline, and prominent direct action buttons: "Call Now", "WhatsApp", and "Find Us on Map".
2. Direct Action CTAs (present in hero, navigation, and floating/sticky mobile bar):
   - WhatsApp message (direct wa.me click-to-chat)
   - Call (direct tel: link)
   - Map (direct Google Maps directions link)
3. No forms or login: No enquiry forms, no WhatsApp enquiry modal, no user accounts, login, or saved passwords.
4. About the Dentist: Background, credentials, experience, philosophy of care, and doctor portfolio.
5. Services & Treatments: Clear breakdown of dental services (e.g., preventive care, cosmetic dentistry, orthodontics, implants, teeth whitening, pediatric dentistry) with clean icons and descriptions.
6. Visual Media & Gallery: Clinic environment photos, treatment room imagery, before-and-after smile transformations, and embedded video presentation/tour placeholders.
7. Clinic Info & Location: Working hours, address, interactive map preview, parking details, and final contact action buttons.
